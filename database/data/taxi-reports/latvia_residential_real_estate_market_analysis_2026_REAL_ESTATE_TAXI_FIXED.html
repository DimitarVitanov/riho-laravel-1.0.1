Latvia Residential Real Estate Market Analysis 2026

Latvia entered 2026 with a strong rebound in residential prices after several weak years. Riga remains the country’s dominant employment, rental and resale market, while Jūrmala, Pierīga and regional cities follow different demand patterns. Lower entry prices can create attractive gross yields, but demographic decline, older Soviet-era stock, energy renovation, geopolitical perception and small-market liquidity require careful selection.

03 · DEMAND AND LIQUIDITY

Demand depends on employment, affordability and property quality

Riga and its commuter belt provide the deepest demand. Regional cities can offer higher headline yields, but their tenant pools and resale liquidity are narrower. Building renovation and apartment-association finances are central because much of the stock is older multi-family housing.

Transaction liquidity should be tested by examining realistic days on market, mortgage eligibility, owner-occupier demand, competing listings and the number of recent comparable sales. A property with more than one buyer profile is generally more resilient.

LIQUIDITY CHECK
  • Employment and population direction
  • Transport and daily services
  • Mortgage eligibility
  • Standard owner-occupier appeal
  • Tenant depth
  • Realistic resale period
04 · SUPPLY AND DEVELOPMENT

National shortage can coexist with local oversupply

New construction should be analysed at district and project level. A national shortage does not protect a building from competition by many similar units delivered nearby. Development feasibility depends on land title, planning, infrastructure, financing, contractor capacity, construction costs and sales absorption.

Existing stock requires a different analysis: building age, deferred maintenance, energy performance, common-area finances and renovation obligations often determine the true cost of ownership.

SUPPLY CHECK
  • Permitted competing projects
  • Unsold completed stock
  • Unit-size competition
  • Construction and completion risk
  • Infrastructure capacity
  • Developer financial strength
05 · RENTAL MARKET

Gross rent is not sustainable net return

Riga

Strongest tenant depth and professional demand.

Regional cities

Higher gross yield can reflect greater vacancy and resale risk.

Older buildings

Heating and renovation contributions reduce net return.

Indicative yield

Net-yield model: deduct vacancy, management, tax, insurance, repairs, furnishing replacement, non-recoverable building charges and major capital expenditure.
06 · MORTGAGE FINANCE

Financing shapes affordability and transaction volume

Mortgage APRC

House-purchase APRC was around 4.34% in April 2026.

Euribor exposure

Most mortgages remain sensitive to euro-area reference rates.

State support

Guarantee programmes can support qualifying households.

Foreign borrowers

Residence, income source and deposit influence bank appetite.

Stress test: use a higher refinancing rate, temporary vacancy, major repair contribution and lower resale price. Bank approval is not proof of investment viability.
07 · FOREIGN BUYERS

Ownership access and practical execution

EU and EEA buyers generally have broad access to Latvian residential property. Restrictions can apply to land categories and certain non-EU buyers. Residence-by-investment rules are separate from ownership and require current legal confirmation.

Foreign buyers should obtain independent local legal, tax and technical advice. Source-of-funds evidence, tax registration, certified translations, power of attorney and mortgage requirements should be resolved before a non-refundable payment.

FOREIGN-BUYER FILE
  • Nationality and residence status
  • Ownership eligibility
  • Tax number and bank account
  • Source-of-funds documents
  • Mortgage eligibility
  • Succession and ownership structure
08 · TAX AND TRANSACTION COST

Purchase price is only one component of total cost

Transfer and registration

State fees and Land Register charges apply to acquisition.

Real estate tax

Municipal tax is based on cadastral value and property category.

Rental income

Latvian rental income is taxable, with available regimes depending on activity and owner status.

Capital gain

Gain taxation and exemptions depend on residence, holding and use.

Budget separately: legal and notarial fees, registry charges, valuation, technical inspection, translation, financing fees, insurance, furnishing and immediate repairs.

16 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Latvia residential property FAQ

What is the current market direction?

The latest quarterly data indicate a sharp national price rebound in Q1 2026. The recovery follows a period of weak or falling real prices and improved mortgage affordability as euro-area interest rates eased.

Can foreigners buy residential property?

EU and EEA buyers generally have broad access to Latvian residential property. Restrictions can apply to land categories and certain non-EU buyers. Residence-by-investment rules are separate from ownership and require current legal confirmation.

What is the main financing risk?

Interest rates, deposit requirements and refinancing conditions can materially change affordability and net return.

Should asking prices be treated as market value?

No. Asking prices must be compared with completed transactions and adjusted for condition, title and micro-location.

What is the largest hidden apartment risk?

Building debt, planned capital works, poor energy performance, unlawful alterations and tenant rights can materially change value.

What should be checked before paying a deposit?

Buyer eligibility, title, encumbrances, planning, finance, technical condition, refund conditions and professional escrow or closing arrangements.
