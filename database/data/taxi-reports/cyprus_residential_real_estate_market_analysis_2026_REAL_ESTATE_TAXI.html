Cyprus Residential Real Estate Market Analysis 2026

Cyprus entered 2026 with accelerating apartment prices, improving mortgage conditions, record tourism, a sharply larger construction pipeline and strong foreign demand concentrated in coastal districts. Yet the island is not one uniform market: Limassol is a high-price international business hub, Nicosia is primarily a domestic owner-occupier and employment market, Larnaca combines relative affordability with airport and regeneration demand, Paphos is heavily foreign- and lifestyle-led, while the government-controlled Famagusta market is smaller and highly seasonal.

EXECUTIVE INTERPRETATION

Demand is broad, but apartment and coastal pressure is doing most of the work

The Central Bank of Cyprus residential index rose 7.5% year-on-year and 2.3% quarter-on-quarter in Q1 2026. Apartments increased 10.8% annually, compared with 3.0% for houses. This is not merely a national affordability story: apartments are the preferred entry point for local households, professionals, relocation demand, international buyers and rental investors, so the same segment is absorbing several demand sources at once.

The strongest overall district growth was in Limassol and Larnaca, followed by Paphos. Nicosia remained positive but more moderate, while Famagusta was flat in the broad index. A buyer should therefore underwrite the exact district, unit type, title status and tenant profile rather than applying the national headline to every property.

Core reading: Cyprus remains demand-led in 2026, but the investable opportunity is narrower than the island-wide story. Legally clean, correctly priced apartments in liquid locations are fundamentally different assets from high-cost villas, off-plan units with delayed titles or properties dependent on one seasonal demand source.
REAL ESTATE TAXI VERDICT

Attractive demand mix, unusually high documentation sensitivity

Euro-area status, corporate relocation, tourism, foreign buyers and lower loan rates support the market. The main discipline is not only valuation: separate title deeds, developer mortgages, deposited sale contracts, planning compliance, VAT treatment and the Republic-controlled location must all be verified before a deposit becomes non-refundable.

06 · SUPPLY PIPELINE

Permitted housing is expanding rapidly, but completion will lag

Cyprus authorised 16,171 dwellings in 2025, up 42.7%, with the largest pipelines in Nicosia, Limassol and Larnaca. The early-2026 acceleration was even stronger: 3,463 residential units were authorised in January–February, 79.2% more than a year earlier.

This should gradually improve choice, but permits are not completed homes. Labour constraints, construction costs, infrastructure, planning conditions, financing and developer execution determine when—or whether—units reach the market.

A city may need housing while one neighbourhood simultaneously receives many similar investor apartments. Check competing projects, delivery dates, parking, common charges and likely tenant depth within the exact micro-market.

09 · TOURISM AND SHORT-STAY DEMAND

Record 2025 arrivals support coastal demand—but do not remove seasonality

Cyprus received 4.534 million tourists in 2025, 12.2% more than in 2024. This supports holiday homes, serviced apartments and coastal rental demand, particularly in Paphos, Famagusta, Larnaca and Limassol.

What tourism supports

Peak-season occupancy, lifestyle demand, international visibility, airport-linked markets and buyer familiarity with the island.

What tourism does not guarantee

Year-round occupancy, legal short-term use, stable nightly rates, low management costs or deep resale demand for every resort project.

Best practice: underwrite a holiday-rental property with a long-term-rent fallback and include management, utilities, platform fees, cleaning, replacement, licence compliance and off-season vacancy.
10 · FOREIGN OWNERSHIP AND RESIDENCY

EU and non-EU buyers follow different acquisition pathways

EU citizens generally purchase ordinary residential property without the third-country permission process. Buyers treated as foreign persons under the Acquisition of Immovable Property (Aliens) Law require consent from the competent District Officer acting under delegated Council of Ministers authority. The official guidance allows a foreign individual or couple to seek permission for a dwelling or, within limits, a plot intended for a residence.

Property ownership and immigration status are separate. Cyprus also maintains a permanent-residence route for qualifying investors, including certain first-sale residential purchases of at least €300,000 plus VAT, but the applicant must meet all income, payment, due-diligence and ongoing eligibility conditions.

Never let a residence-marketing promise replace independent immigration and property-law advice. Approval to reside, permission to acquire and clean legal title are separate questions.

12 · TITLE DEEDS AND SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE

A missing separate title is not automatically fatal—but it changes the risk

Cyprus has a long history of transactions where a buyer signs before a separate title deed is issued. In such cases, the sale contract should be deposited with the Department of Lands and Surveys within six months. This activates the Sale of Immovable Property (Specific Performance) Law and helps prevent the seller from disposing of the same property contrary to the buyer’s rights.

For contracts concluded after 12 December 2023, the seller must include a search certificate—dated within five working days of the contract—showing encumbrances and prohibitions. The amended framework also provides mechanisms for payment to a mortgagee and transfer despite certain seller encumbrances, but the practical structure must be reviewed by an independent lawyer.

1Search

Obtain title, mortgage, memo, prohibition, planning and ownership information before commitment.

2Contract

Use a precise sale contract identifying the unit, plans, price, delivery, title obligations and remedies.

3Deposit

Deposit the contract with DLS within six months; the current filing fee is €50.

4Transfer

Complete tax clearances, lender releases, permissions and title transfer when conditions are satisfied.

Do not use the seller’s or developer’s lawyer as your only adviser.The buyer’s lawyer should be independent, verify every encumbrance and explain what happens if the separate title is delayed, refused or issued with unauthorised works.

15 · REPUBLIC-CONTROLLED AREA VS NORTHERN AREA

This distinction is fundamental, not a normal district comparison

The official data, ownership process, Lands and Surveys protections, VAT treatment and transfer rules in this report apply to areas under the effective control of the Republic of Cyprus. Transactions in the northern area may involve land claimed by displaced owners, court judgments, criminal-law exposure and limited international recognition of local title arrangements.

Extreme caution is required.Do not treat a lower advertised price in the northern area as equivalent to a discounted Republic of Cyprus property. Obtain specialist independent legal advice with experience in pre-1974 title claims and cross-border enforcement before paying any money.

Lower financing costs and foreign demand remain supportive, while expanding supply gradually slows apartment price growth from the Q1 pace.

20 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Cyprus residential property FAQ

How fast were Cyprus prices rising in Q1 2026?

The CBC overall residential index rose 7.5% year-on-year and 2.3% quarter-on-quarter.

Which segment was strongest?

Apartments increased 10.8% annually, compared with 3.0% for houses. Demand for smaller, rentable and internationally marketable units was stronger.

Which district had the strongest overall growth?

Limassol led at 9.1% year-on-year, narrowly ahead of Larnaca at 8.9%. Paphos recorded 6.4%, Nicosia 2.8% and Famagusta 0.0%.

Can foreigners buy property in Cyprus?

EU citizens generally buy ordinary residential property directly. Third-country buyers usually require District Officer consent under the Aliens acquisition law and should confirm the permitted property type and extent.

Do I pay transfer fees on a VAT new build?

No transfer fee is charged where VAT applies to the same property transaction. Non-VAT transactions generally receive a 50% reduction on the calculated transfer fee, subject to exceptions.

What should I do if there is no separate title deed?

Use an independent lawyer, obtain the required encumbrance certificate, negotiate a detailed contract and deposit it with the Lands Office within six months to activate specific-performance protection.

Is a 4.88% yield net?

No. It is an indicative gross yield. Net return is lower after vacancy, common expenses, maintenance, insurance, management, utilities, tax and replacement costs.

Does this analysis include northern Cyprus?

No. It covers Republic-controlled areas. The northern area has a materially different and potentially severe title-risk environment.
