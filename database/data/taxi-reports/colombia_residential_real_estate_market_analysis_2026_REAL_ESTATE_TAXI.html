03

3. Official new-home prices

DANE reported a 2.79% quarter-on-quarter increase in new-dwelling prices in Q1 2026. This is official new-home evidence and should not be presented as a completed-sale index for all existing homes.

04

4. Sales momentum

The cited sector data showed new-home sales 11.7% higher year on year through March 2026, while construction starts were at a 14-year low. Stronger sales with weak starts can support future supply discipline, but delivery pipelines vary by city.

05

5. Inflation and rates

Annual inflation was 6.14% in June 2026 and Banco de la República’s policy rate was 12.0% from July 1, 2026. High rates affect affordability, developer finance and capitalization rates.

06

6. Bogotá

Bogotá offers the largest employment and tenant market but large differences exist between neighborhoods, socioeconomic strata, commute times and building age.

07

7. Medellín

Medellín combines local demand, tourism and foreign-buyer interest. Short-term rental legality, condominium rules and neighborhood-level saturation require careful checking.

08

8. Cartagena and coastal markets

Tourism can support rents, but humidity, corrosion, seasonality, insurance and high administration fees can reduce net returns.

09

9. Foreign buyers

Foreigners can generally acquire property, but clean title, source-of-funds compliance, tax registration and foreign-exchange documentation are important.

10

10. Taxes and transaction costs

Notary, registry, withholding, legal, appraisal, brokerage and municipal charges should be itemized before signing.

11

11. Off-plan risk

Verify developer solvency, fiduciary structure, permits, delivery conditions, refund remedies and whether advertised amenities are approved.

12

12. 2026–2028 scenario

Base case: positive nominal growth but limited real appreciation under high inflation and rates. Upside requires rate relief; downside includes affordability stress and stalled projects.

13

13. Market structure

This report separates official data, asking-market evidence, third-party estimates and Real Estate Taxi analysis. No asking price is presented as a completed transaction unless the source explicitly identifies it as such.

14

14. Regional comparison

National averages can hide very different city, coastal, inland, luxury and affordable-market conditions. Every investment should be underwritten at micro-location and building level.

15

15. New-build versus resale

New-build property may offer modern specifications but creates developer, permit and delivery risk. Resale property can be inspected immediately but may contain deferred maintenance and legal alterations.

16

16. Transaction volume and liquidity

Liquidity should be evaluated through verified completed sales, days on market, negotiation discounts and buyer depth. Portal inventory alone is insufficient.

17

17. Supply and construction

Permits, starts and completions are different statistics. New supply must be compared with actual absorption and unsold inventory.

18

18. Long-term rental underwriting

Use realistic achieved rent, vacancy, management, repairs, tax, insurance and building expenses. Gross yield is only a first screening metric.

19

19. Short-term rental underwriting

Check legality, building rules, tax registration, seasonality, platform fees, cleaning, furnishing and a long-term-rental fallback case.

20

20. Affordability

Compare prices and mortgage payments with local household income, not only foreign purchasing power. Affordability affects resale and tenant depth.

21

21. Insurance and climate

Obtain property-specific insurance terms before closing. Hazard exposure and exclusions can materially change net return.

22

22. Investor suitability

The market may suit long-term, relocation, income or lifestyle buyers differently. A strong country story cannot compensate for weak title, bad pricing or poor building condition.

23

23. Detailed due-diligence checklist

Confirm owner identity, title, liens, cadastral area, taxes, permits, utilities, physical condition, insurance, condominium debts, rental rules, payment security and exit liquidity.

24

24. Methodology

Statistics are dated and classified by source type. Real Estate Taxi scenarios, risk ratings and net-yield ranges are analytical interpretations rather than guarantees or formal valuations.
